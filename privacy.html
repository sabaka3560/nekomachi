Privacy policy

Nekomachi: Rooftop Cat · last updated 27 October 2026

Nekomachi is a free browser and Android game made by Tanmay Bohra. It does not ask for an account, does not show ads on cats.tanmaybohra.com or in the Android app, and does not use analytics or tracking.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress: best score and distance, fish, cats, outfits, worlds, sounds and upgrades you unlocked, daily missions and login streak.
  • Settings such as sound and graphics quality, and which tips you have already seen.
  • A random player id (a string of letters and numbers, not linked to you) and the runner name you choose for the leaderboard.

These are kept in your browser's storage (in the Android app, the storage of the app's browser). Clearing the site's data or uninstalling the app deletes them. Sounds and game files are cached so the game starts quickly and works offline.

What is sent, and only if you choose to

The world leaderboard is optional. If you enter a runner name, then at the end of a run the game sends your runner name, score, distance, run length, number of fish and buildings smashed, the cat you ran as, and your random player id to api.tanmaybohra.com. Your runner name, score, distance and cat are shown publicly on the leaderboard. You can untick “Put my best runs on the world leaderboard” at any time, and nothing more is sent.

Like any web server, the API briefly processes your IP address to deliver responses and to limit how often scores can be posted. It is not stored with your scores.

Things we do not collect

No email, phone number, contacts, location, photos, microphone, camera or device identifiers. No data is sold or shared with advertisers.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The only free text is the runner name, which is filtered for offensive words; please do not use your real full name.

Removing your leaderboard entry

Email hello@tanmaybohra.com with your runner name and roughly when you played, and the entry will be removed.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.
